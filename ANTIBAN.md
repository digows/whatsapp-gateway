# Pacing and typing

The [gateway use case](docs/use-cases/gateway-core.md#pacing-typing-and-shutdown) owns the current
bounded pacing, typing and shutdown behavior. These are operational and user-experience controls.
They do not guarantee that WhatsApp will permit or continue an account/session.

The former behavior-variation, fingerprint and health-score descriptions are not implemented or
promised by this reconstruction. No contact policy or application business rules belong here.
